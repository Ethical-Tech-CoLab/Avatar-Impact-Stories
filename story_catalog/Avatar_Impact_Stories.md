# Avatar Impact Stories: Many Voices

## Project Overview

"Many Voices" is an interactive kiosk installation featuring AI-generated avatars that deliver survivor testimonies. The project surfaces first-person impact stories told through synthetic avatars to protect survivor identities while preserving the authenticity of their accounts.

**Design Philosophy:** Publishing a real face alongside an account of trafficking or forced labour can expose someone to retaliation from the very people they escaped. The synthetic avatars serve a protective function while maintaining the power and truth of first-person testimony.

**Technology:** Avatars were produced using D-ID technology to generate photorealistic presenters from still images and voice tracks. Stories typically run 40 seconds to 2 minutes.

**Content Warning:** Stories describe trafficking, forced and child labour, and violence.
